# Activity 6 — Coach a Worried Team Member (Role-Play Simulator)

**Purpose:** Practise coaching a colleague who fears AI will replace their job, using the GROW model, and co-create a redesigned role that supervises AI agents.

## What you need

- A web browser
- The simulator in this folder: **`role-play-simulator/index.html`**
- A **training** OpenAI or MiniMax API key (from your trainer)
- The primer in this folder: `COACHING-GUIDE.md`

**Evidence status: SIM — all data is fictional.** The team members and their situations are made up for practice.

> **API key safety:** Use only the **low-limit training key** your trainer provides. **Never** use a production or personal key. The key is stored **only in your browser** for this session and is not sent anywhere else. Clear it when you finish.

## Steps

1. Open **`role-play-simulator/index.html`** in your browser.
2. Paste the **training** API key when prompted (OpenAI or MiniMax, as your trainer directs).
3. Pick one scenario:
   - **Sarah** — marketing, worried the agent writes all the copy now.
   - **David** — customer service, worried the chatbot handles all the easy queries.
   - **Mei Ling** — data analyst, worried the agent does the spreadsheets faster than she can.
4. Coach with **empathy** using the **GROW** model (Goal, Reality, Options, Will) — see `COACHING-GUIDE.md`. Listen first; do not jump to solutions.
5. Together, **co-create a redesigned role** where the person **supervises and checks** the AI agent — reviewing output, catching errors, owning the final decision.
6. Add the new skills the person would need (for example, spotting AI mistakes, prompt-writing, approving agent actions).
7. Click **"Get Coach Feedback"** and read how you did. Adjust and try one more exchange if time allows.

## What you produce

- One coaching conversation using all four GROW stages
- A redesigned role description for your chosen team member (what they supervise, what they own)
- A short list of new "agent-management" skills for that person

## Reflect (Data Privacy / Job Impact / Ethical Concerns / Cyber Security)

- **Data Privacy:** In their new role, what data does the person now oversee the agent handling?
- **Job Impact:** What did the person lose to the agent, and what more valuable work did they gain?
- **Ethical Concerns:** Is it fair to expect staff to supervise a tool that could replace them? How do you support them?
- **Cyber Security:** Why does a human checking the agent's output also make the system safer?
