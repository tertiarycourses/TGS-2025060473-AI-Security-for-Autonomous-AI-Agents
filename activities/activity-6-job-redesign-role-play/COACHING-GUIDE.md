# Coaching Guide — GROW & Job Redesign

**Evidence status: SIM — all data is fictional.** A one-page primer for the role-play in Activity 6.

## The GROW model

A simple, four-stage way to coach someone. Move through it in order, and listen more than you talk.

| Stage | What it means | Ask something like… |
|-------|---------------|---------------------|
| **G — Goal** | What does the person want? | "If this went well, what would your job look like in six months?" |
| **R — Reality** | What is actually happening now? | "What is the agent doing today that used to be yours?" |
| **O — Options** | What could they do about it? | "What parts of your work could you own *more* if the agent handled the routine bits?" |
| **W — Will** | What will they commit to? | "What is one thing you'll try this week, and who can support you?" |

## Job-redesign framework

Use this once the person feels heard. Redesign the role *around* the agent, not against it.

1. **List the tasks** in the person's current role.
2. **Mark the agent-doable ones** — repetitive, high-volume, rule-based tasks.
3. **Redesign the human role around judgement** — checking, deciding, handling exceptions, the human touch with customers.
4. **Add agent-management skills** — spotting AI errors, writing good prompts, approving or rejecting agent actions, owning the final call.
5. **Retrain** — agree what to learn and how the organisation will support it.

## 5 good coaching phrases

1. "Tell me more about what's worrying you."
2. "That makes sense — a lot of people feel that way at first."
3. "What part of your work do you most enjoy that a machine can't do?"
4. "Where could you add value by checking and improving what the agent produces?"
5. "What support would make this feel manageable?"

## 5 phrases to avoid

1. "Don't worry, it's fine." *(dismisses the fear)*
2. "You'll just have to adapt." *(no support offered)*
3. "The agent is better at this than you anyway." *(demoralising)*
4. "Everyone's job is changing, deal with it." *(cold, generic)*
5. "Here's exactly what you should do…" *(jumps to solutions before listening)*
