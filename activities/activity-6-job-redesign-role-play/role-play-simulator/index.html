AI Adoption Coaching Role-Play

Practise coaching a staff member who fears AI agents will take their job. The AI plays the staff member — you play the manager.

How this activity works

  • Pick a scenario below. The AI will stay in character as that staff member.
  • Your job as the manager/coach: hold a supportive career conversation about working with AI agents, not being replaced by them.
  • When you are done (aim for 6–10 exchanges), click Get Coach Feedback for a scored GROW-model review of your coaching.
What good coaching looks like: acknowledge feelings first (empathy before solutions) · ask open questions to understand their reality · explore their strengths · co-create a concrete redesigned role — e.g. AI agent supervisor, prompt quality reviewer, exception handler, human escalation point. The staff member opens up only if they feel heard. Dismissing feelings ("don't worry lah", "just upskill") or threatening keeps them resistant.
