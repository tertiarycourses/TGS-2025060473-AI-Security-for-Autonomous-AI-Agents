## Main task — the REPORT prompt

Send this as one prompt (adjust wording if your trainer tells you to):

> Analyse the uploaded Excel file of monthly marketing data for Sunset Bay Resort Singapore (Jan–Jun 2026, Google Ads and Facebook Ads).
>
> 1. **Analyse:** For each channel, calculate total Spend, total Revenue, ROI (Revenue ÷ Spend), conversion rate (Conversions ÷ Clicks) and cost per conversion (Spend ÷ Conversions). Also show the month-on-month revenue trend for each channel.
> 2. **Charts:** Generate three charts — (a) monthly Revenue by channel as a line chart, (b) ROI by channel as a bar chart, (c) Spend vs Revenue by month for Facebook Ads.
> 3. **Report:** Generate a **Word (.docx) report** titled "Sunset Bay Resort — Marketing Performance Report, Jan–Jun 2026" containing: an executive summary (max 5 sentences, plain English for a non-technical resort manager), the metrics table, the three charts each with a one-sentence caption, a short analysis of what the numbers show, and a **Recommendations** section with at least three specific, numbers-backed recommendations (including one channel to reduce spend on and why).
>
> Give me the .docx as a downloadable file.

## Report checklist (mark as you check the .docx)

- [ ] Executive summary is plain English and 5 sentences or fewer
- [ ] Metrics table present, and the totals match the spreadsheet
- [ ] All three charts present, each with a caption
- [ ] Analysis mentions that Facebook Ads spend keeps rising while its revenue flattens near S$30,000
- [ ] At least three recommendations, each backed by a number from the data
- [ ] Nothing invented — every figure traceable to the spreadsheet
