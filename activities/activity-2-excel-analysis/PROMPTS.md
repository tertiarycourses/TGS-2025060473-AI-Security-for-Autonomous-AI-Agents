# Activity 2 — Bad vs Good Prompts

**Evidence status: SIM — all data is fictional.** Use with `MOCK-MARKETING-DATA.md` in the Hermes (MiniMax) agent. Send the BAD prompt first, then the GOOD one, and compare.

## Task 1 — Top channels by ROI

- **Bad:** "Which is better?"
- **Good:** "Using the table, calculate ROI as Revenue ÷ Spend for each Channel across all six months combined. Rank the channels from highest to lowest ROI and show the result as a small table with Channel, Total Spend, Total Revenue and ROI."

## Task 2 — Month-on-month trend

- **Bad:** "Show me the trend."
- **Good:** "For Google Ads only, list Revenue for each month Jan to Jun and show the month-on-month change as a percentage. Tell me in one sentence whether the trend is rising, flat or falling."

## Task 3 — An underperformer to cut

- **Bad:** "What should we stop doing?"
- **Good:** "Compare how Revenue changed against Spend for each Channel from Jan to Jun. Identify one channel where Spend keeps rising but Revenue is flattening, name it, and give the two numbers that prove it."

## Task 4 — One-paragraph executive summary

- **Bad:** "Summarise this."
- **Good:** "Write a 4-sentence executive summary for a resort manager (non-technical). Cover: which channel gives the best return, the overall six-month revenue trend, one channel to reduce spend on, and one clear recommendation. Plain English, no jargon."
