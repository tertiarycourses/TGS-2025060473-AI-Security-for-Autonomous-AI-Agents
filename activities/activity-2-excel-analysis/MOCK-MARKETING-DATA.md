## Helpful hints for analysis

- **ROI** here can be read as **Revenue ÷ Spend** (a return-on-spend ratio), or as **(Revenue − Spend) ÷ Spend** for a percentage return. Tell the agent which one you mean.
- **Conversion rate** = Conversions ÷ Clicks.
- **Cost per conversion** = Spend ÷ Conversions.
- Google Ads spend and revenue both grow steadily month on month; Facebook Ads spend keeps rising but revenue flattens around S$30,000 — a useful clue for the "underperformer to cut" task.
