# Activity 5 - Agent Deployment Gate Checklist

## Organisational accountability

- [ ] Business, technical, security, privacy, model-risk and operational owners are named.
- [ ] Leadership has approved the use case, risk tolerance and non-agent alternative.
- [ ] Users are told when an agent acts, what it can access and how to challenge outcomes.

## Bound autonomy

- [ ] Each capability is classified by impact, reversibility, data sensitivity and likelihood.
- [ ] Read, recommend, prepare and execute permissions are separated.
- [ ] Irreversible, legal, financial, privacy and safety actions have deterministic approval or are prohibited.
- [ ] Approval shows exact fields, recipient, amount, target and reason and expires after one action.
- [ ] Agent identity, credentials, tools, skills/plugins, recipients, cost, retries and duration are bounded.

## Fairness, reliability and privacy

- [ ] Accuracy, false escalation and outcomes meet per-segment floors and disparity limits.
- [ ] Hallucination and unsafe-action rates are converted into expected annual affected cases.
- [ ] Prompt, output, memory, tool and log personal-data surfaces are inventoried and minimised.
- [ ] PDPA purpose, notice/consent where applicable, protection, transfer, retention and rights are evidenced.

## Responsible AI assurance

- [ ] The board has tested both attacker-driven misuse and foreseeable harmful use without an attacker.
- [ ] Outcome, denial, escalation and false-positive results are segmented by affected group.
- [ ] Users receive clear notice of agent use, material limitations, data/tool reach and the human escalation route.
- [ ] Affected people can obtain human review, correction and remedy for consequential outcomes.
- [ ] Provider, builder, deployer, operator, approver and end-user responsibilities are documented without gaps.
- [ ] Independent or second-line review is required where impact exceeds the organisation's defined threshold.

## Operations

- [ ] Monitoring covers identity, tool sequences, approvals, overrides, egress, cost and policy denials.
- [ ] Kill switch, rollback, credential revocation and manual fallback are tested.
- [ ] Incident response includes the DPO, evidence preservation and breach-notification assessment.
- [ ] Model, corpus, tool, skill/plugin or policy changes automatically trigger re-testing.
- [ ] Residual risks have owners, mitigations and expiry dates.

## Board decision

- [ ] Go.
- [ ] Go with explicit conditions and expiry.
- [ ] No-go; the use case cannot be bounded within risk tolerance.
