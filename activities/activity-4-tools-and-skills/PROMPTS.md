# Activity 4 Prompts — Same Prompt, Before and After the Skill

**Evidence status: SIM — all data is fictional.** You will run the **same 10-slide prompt twice**: once **before** installing the `pptx` skill (Deck A) and once **after** (Deck B). Copy it exactly both times — the prompt must not change, only the skill.

## The 10-slide prompt (run it twice, word for word)

> "Build a **10-slide** PowerPoint for the fictional **Sunset Bay Resort — 2026 Marketing Plan**, expanding the 5-slide script from my earlier task. Return it as a downloadable PowerPoint file.
> Exactly 10 slides, in this order:
> 1. **Title** — Sunset Bay Resort — 2026 Marketing Plan (subtitle: Growing direct bookings for our fictional beachfront resort)
> 2. **Agenda** — one line per section of this deck
> 3. **Where We Are Today** — Google Ads returns about S$10 per S$1 spent; Facebook Ads spend rising but bookings flat since March; guests love the sea-view rooms and weekend brunch
> 4. **Our Guests** — two short fictional guest profiles (a young couple on a weekend break; a family on school-holiday travel)
> 5. **Our 2026 Goals** — grow direct bookings 20%; shift budget to the best-returning channels; launch a simple loyalty offer
> 6. **Marketing Channels** — increase Google Ads gradually; reduce Facebook Ads; test email offers to past guests
> 7. **Campaign Calendar** — one simple quarter-by-quarter plan (Q1 loyalty launch, Q2 school-holiday push, Q3 review-themed ad copy, Q4 year-end offer)
> 8. **Budget & Expected Return** — keep every figure consistent with slide 3; track cost per booking monthly
> 9. **Risks & How We Manage Them** — 2–3 simple risks (e.g. channel costs rise, offer misused) each with one plain mitigation
> 10. **Summary** — the 4 key takeaways
> Rules: **2–4 short bullets** per content slide; **do not invent figures** beyond what I gave you; **brand tone:** warm, welcoming, confident — a friendly beachfront resort, not a bank; add a simple **title animation** to every slide."
