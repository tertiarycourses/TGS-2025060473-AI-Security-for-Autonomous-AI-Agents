## Slide 1 — Title

**Sunset Bay Resort — 2026 Marketing Plan**
Subtitle: Growing direct bookings for our fictional beachfront resort
Presenter: Marketing Team · August 2026

## Slide 2 — Where We Are Today

- Google Ads is our strongest channel, returning around S$10 for every S$1 spent.
- Facebook Ads spend keeps rising, but bookings have flattened since March.
- Guests love the sea-view rooms and the weekend brunch — our best review themes.

## Slide 3 — Our 2026 Goals

- Grow direct website bookings by 20% over the year.
- Shift budget toward the channels with the best return.
- Launch a simple loyalty offer for repeat guests.

## Slide 4 — How We Get There

- Increase Google Ads budget gradually and track cost per booking each month.
- Reduce Facebook Ads spend and test one new channel (email offers to past guests).
- Use guest review themes to write warmer, more specific ad copy.

## Slide 5 — Summary

- Focus spend where returns are highest.
- Cut what is flattening; test one new idea.
- Target: +20% direct bookings by end of 2026.
- One owner reviews the numbers every month.
